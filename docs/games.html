Games, one layer deeper

How game companies turn players into revenue, what Take-Two is betting on GTA VI, why Roblox lost half its value, and how the memory shortage is pushing up console prices.

AHow games make money BGTA VI & Take-Two CRoblox DConsoles EThe rest
If you only read one part

The short version

  • Games is a roughly $214 billion market, and phones are more than half of it. Consoles and PCs split the rest.
  • GTA VI launches Thursday at $79.99. Take-Two expects holiday-quarter bookings of $3.35–3.50B, about double last quarter. Analysts expect anywhere from 35 to 70 million copies in the first year.
  • Roblox is down about half in a year. Mandatory age checks made the platform safer but cut chat, sign-ups and spending. Bookings are falling 10–15% a year.
  • Consoles are getting pricier, not cheaper. The AI-driven memory shortage from sector 2 pushed the Switch 2 to $499.99, and Sony calls memory costs its main risk for next year.

Take-Two's fiscal year ends March 31, so the GTA VI quarter is its fiscal Q3, reported in early February. It kept its full-year bookings outlook at $8.0–8.2B.

  • The stock fell before launch. Take-Two dropped about 5–6% today, three days before release, and trades around 5.6 times sales. Traders call this "buy the rumor, sell the news": the excitement is already in the price, so a great launch may only meet expectations.

  • The long tail is the real prize. GTA V earned for over a decade through GTA Online. Investors will watch whether GTA VI's online mode can do the same, which matters more than opening weekend.

Part C

Roblox RBLX

How Roblox makes money

  1. Players buy Robux, Roblox's in-game currency, with real money.

  2. They spend Robux inside games made by independent creators.

  3. Roblox keeps a share and pays creators through its DevEx program: $403M last quarter, 24% of bookings, up from 22% after it raised creator rates.

  4. Revenue is spread out over time, which is why Roblox's revenue can rise while its bookings fall.

Consoles: Nintendo, Sony, Xbox

Console makers usually sell hardware at thin margins, then earn on games, subscriptions and the cut they take from other publishers. This year the hardware got harder: the memory shortage covered in sector 2 raised the cost of every console.

A link between sectors. The same HBM boom that made SK Hynix record profits made ordinary memory scarce. That's why a console launched last year now costs $50 more. Sectors that look unrelated often share a supply chain.

Words in this sector

Net bookings
What players spent in a period, before accounting spreads it out. The headline number for game companies.
Deferred revenue
Money already paid but not yet counted as revenue, because the company still owes players the service.
DAU
Daily active users. Roblox's most watched number.
Payers
Users who spend money in a month. Usually a small fraction of all players.
DevEx
Roblox's program for converting creators' Robux into real money.
Live service
A game updated for years with new content and purchases, like GTA Online.
Sell-in vs. sell-through
Units shipped to stores versus units bought by players. Companies often report sell-in.
Platform fee
The cut a console maker or app store takes, usually around 30%.
Check yourself

Seven-question quiz

Score: 0 of 7 answered correctly

1. Which part of the games market is biggest?

Mobile is about 57% of the market, mostly from free-to-play games with in-game purchases.

2. What are "net bookings"?

Bookings show current demand. Revenue can lag because companies spread recognition over the time they serve players.

3. Take-Two fell about 5–6% just before the biggest launch in its history. What's the most likely reason?

"Buy the rumor, sell the news." The forecast was kept and pre-orders were described as unprecedented. Expectations were simply high.

4. Roblox's revenue rose 16% while its bookings fell 12%. How can both be true?

Revenue reflects money paid in earlier, stronger months. Bookings show spending is falling now, which is why investors focus on it.

5. Why did mandatory age checks hurt Roblox's numbers?

Chat and friends drive engagement on Roblox. Restricting chat until a check is done made the platform safer but slowed growth.

6. Why did the Switch 2 get more expensive a year after launch?

AI's demand for HBM made ordinary memory scarce and expensive, and tariffs added cost. Nintendo raised the US price by $50.

7. Who usually keeps about 30% of a game sold on a console?

Sony, Microsoft and Nintendo take a platform fee on games sold for their consoles, similar to app stores on phones.
