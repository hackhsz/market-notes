Part B

How much news can a stock absorb?

Prices move on the gap between what happened and what was already expected. A great report can still drop a stock if everyone expected greatness. Use the simulator to feel how expectations, guidance, the run-up and company size shape the reaction.

1 · SurpriseExpectations, not news

A beat that matches the unofficial "whisper" is no surprise at all. Only the part nobody expected moves the price.

2 · Implied moveThe options market's guess

Option prices imply how big a move traders are paying for. Nvidia's is about ±9% tonight. It's a size, not a direction.

3 · GuidanceThe future beats the past

Next quarter's forecast usually moves a stock more than the quarter just reported.

4 · Run-upCrowded trades

If a stock rose a lot into the news, many holders are waiting to sell on it. That's "buy the rumor, sell the news."

5 · SizeBig stocks absorb, small ones swing

Giant companies have so many buyers and sellers that news moves them less. Small stocks and 2× funds overshoot both ways.

6 · Follow-throughThe first move isn't final

A move backed by better guidance and heavy volume often keeps drifting. A move on hype tends to fade within days.

    Part C

    Real reactions: Nvidia's last 8 reports

    The model is tidy; reality isn't. Here's how Nvidia and SK Hynix (Seoul shares) actually moved after each report. SK Hynix is often treated as an "Nvidia follower," but it moved the same direction as Nvidia only 3 times out of 8.

    Nvidia reportNvidia next daySK Hynix next Seoul daySK Hynix after 3 days
    Nov 20, 2024+0.5%−1.1%+3.8%
    Feb 26, 2025−8.5%−1.9%−8.3%
    May 28, 2025+3.2%+1.9%−0.2%
    Aug 27, 2025−0.8%+3.3%−1.5%
    Nov 19, 2025−3.2%+1.6%−7.5%
    Feb 25, 2026−5.5%+8.0%−7.8%
    May 20, 2026*−1.8%+11.2%+17.6%
    Aug 26, 2026+8.7%+2.5%−0.8%

    Computed from daily closing prices (Yahoo Finance). Nvidia reports after the US close, so SK Hynix's "next Seoul day" is the Korean session that opens a few hours later. *May date approximate. Nvidia's average next-day move was about 4%, even though the options market typically prices more.

    Three lessons from the table. The first-day direction often reversed within 3 days. Followers don't reliably follow. And the biggest SK Hynix gains came after Nvidia fell, when investors focused on memory demand instead of Nvidia's own numbers.
